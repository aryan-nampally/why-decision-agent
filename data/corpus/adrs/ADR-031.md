# ADR-031: Invoice generation as a nightly batch

## Context

Invoices are generated per shipment once it is delivered. Today a cron job in the monolith builds them one by one throughout the day, and it competes with booking traffic for database connections.

Customers currently accept invoices the morning after delivery, and no customer contract requires same-day invoicing.

Nightly volume is about 120,000 invoices, and a prototype batch job finishes in 2 hours 10 minutes of the 01:00–04:00 UTC maintenance window.

## Considered Options

1. **Nightly batch** on a dedicated worker pool during the maintenance window
2. **Event-driven invoicing** triggered by the delivered event on the event backbone

## Decision Outcome

Chosen option: **nightly batch.** Event-driven invoicing was rejected for now because billing's tax engine integration is not idempotent, and replayed events would risk duplicate invoices.

## Consequences

- Good: invoice generation no longer competes with daytime booking load.
- Bad: invoices are up to a day late by design.
