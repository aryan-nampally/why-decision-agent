# ADR-015: Event backbone for shipment tracking events

## Context

Shipment status changes (picked up, in transit, delayed, delivered) are currently pushed through a chain of SNS topics and SQS queues. Three consumers exist: tracking, billing, and the customer notification service.

Tracking, billing and notifications all need to replay the last seven days of shipment events after a bad deploy; last month a billing bug required a manual re-send of 2.3 million events from database snapshots, which took a weekend.

Two engineers on the platform team, Marcus Oyelaran and Jin Park, have run Kafka in production at previous companies, so we would not be starting from zero on operations.

## Considered Options

1. **Amazon MSK (managed Kafka)**, 7-day retention, 12 partitions per topic
2. **Keep SNS/SQS** and add an S3 archive for replay
3. **Kinesis Data Streams**

## Decision Outcome

Chosen option: **Amazon MSK with 7-day retention.**

SNS/SQS with an S3 archive was rejected because replay would still be a bespoke job per consumer. Kinesis was rejected because extended retention is priced per shard-hour and consumer fan-out is limited without enhanced fan-out.

## Consequences

- Good: any consumer can rewind its offset to replay history.
- Bad: Kafka has sharp operational edges (rebalancing, partition skew); we are relying on in-team experience to run it.
