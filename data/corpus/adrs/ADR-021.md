# ADR-021: Identity provider for shipper and staff logins

## Context

Shipper portal logins are handled by a homegrown session service with bcrypt passwords. We need SSO for enterprise shippers, MFA for staff, and OIDC for the mobile app.

We have about 18,000 monthly active users across the shipper portal and mobile app, and the growth forecast stays under 50,000 through 2025.

None of our current customer contracts require SOC 2 attestation, so we accept that Keycloak's default audit logging would need extra work before it could satisfy an auditor.

## Considered Options

1. **Self-hosted Keycloak** on EKS
2. **Auth0** (Professional tier)
3. **AWS Cognito**

## Decision Outcome

Chosen option: **self-hosted Keycloak.**

Auth0 was rejected on cost: at our MAU it is about $2,300 per month and rises steeply past 50k MAU. Cognito was rejected because its SAML federation and hosted UI customisation were too limited for enterprise SSO.

## Consequences

- Good: no per-user licensing; full control over login flows.
- Bad: we operate Keycloak upgrades and its Postgres database ourselves.
- Bad: audit trails for admin actions are not compliance-grade out of the box.
