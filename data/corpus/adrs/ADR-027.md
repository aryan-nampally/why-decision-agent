# ADR-027: Retry policy for carrier API calls

## Context

Booking, tracking and label generation all call external carrier APIs (Northline Carriers, BlueRidge Logistics, Pacific Coast Freight). Roughly 1.8% of calls fail, and each failure surfaces to the user as "carrier unavailable, try again."

Looking at 30 days of logs, failures from carrier APIs are almost always transient, such as network blips and brief 503s during carrier deploys, and succeed on a second or third attempt.

Our heaviest carrier, Northline, allows 100 requests per second per account; our peak traffic to Northline is about 60 requests per second, so there is plenty of headroom for retries.

## Considered Options

1. **Exponential backoff** (base 200 ms, factor 2, up to 5 retries) in the shared carrier client
2. **No retries**, surface errors to the user
3. **Queue failed calls** and complete them asynchronously

## Decision Outcome

Chosen option: **exponential backoff with up to 5 retries, applied in the shared carrier client library.**

No-retry was rejected because it pushes transient noise onto users. Async queuing was rejected for booking because the user is waiting for a label.

## Consequences

- Good: in replay tests, user-visible carrier errors drop from 1.8% to 0.1%.
- Bad: worst-case latency for a failing call rises to about 6 seconds.
