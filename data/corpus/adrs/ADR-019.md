# ADR-019: Synchronous REST between booking and pricing

## Context

When a shipper confirms a booking we must re-price the shipment against current rate cards before charging. The question is whether booking should call pricing synchronously or request a price asynchronously over the event backbone.

Pricing's /quote endpoint has had a p99 of 95 ms over the last quarter, and the booking flow's budget for the pricing call is 150 ms.

Pricing has exactly two synchronous consumers today, booking and the customer portal, so an extra synchronous caller does not create a fan-in risk.

## Considered Options

1. **Synchronous REST call** from booking to pricing with a 150 ms timeout and one retry
2. **Asynchronous request/reply over Kafka**

## Decision Outcome

Chosen option: **synchronous REST with a 150 ms timeout.**

The asynchronous option was rejected because the user is waiting on the confirmation screen, and request/reply over Kafka adds latency and correlation complexity for no benefit at current latencies.

## Consequences

- Good: simple, easy to trace, consistent price at booking time.
- Bad: booking availability now depends on pricing availability.
