# ADR-012: Caching strategy for the rate-quote API

## Context

The rate-quote API computes a freight price from carrier rate cards, fuel surcharges and lane history. Uncached, a quote takes 180–400 ms because it fans out to three carrier rate services. The shipper portal shows quotes as the user types a destination, so latency is visible.

All of our shippers today are in the United States and Canada, and the product requirement is a p95 quote latency under 40 ms measured from North America.

Carrier rate cards change at most a few times per day. Sales Engineering confirmed that serving a quote up to five minutes old is acceptable to customers, because the final price is re-validated at booking.

## Considered Options

1. **Single ElastiCache Redis cluster in us-east-1**, cache key = lane + weight band + service level, TTL 5 minutes
2. **Per-region Redis with cross-region replication**
3. **CDN edge caching of quote responses**

## Decision Outcome

Chosen option: **single-region Redis in us-east-1 with a 5-minute TTL.**

Per-region caches were rejected as unnecessary complexity while every customer is in North America. CDN caching was rejected because quotes are personalised by contract discounts.

## Consequences

- Good: p95 drops to about 12 ms for cache hits in load tests; hit rate about 85%.
- Bad: a regional outage in us-east-1 takes quoting down entirely.
- Bad: the cache key does not include currency; all prices are USD today.
