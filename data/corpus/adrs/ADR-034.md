# ADR-034: Managed feature-flag service

## Context

Feature flags are currently environment variables, which means a deploy for every toggle. Product wants percentage rollouts and per-customer targeting.

We run about 180 flags today. The vendor's Growth tier covers up to 500 active flags at $1,200 per month, and Finance approved that spend in the FY25 platform budget.

## Considered Options

1. **Managed flag service** (Growth tier)
2. **Self-hosted Unleash**

## Decision Outcome

Chosen option: **managed flag service on the Growth tier.** Self-hosting was rejected because the platform team is already stretched operating Kafka and Keycloak.

## Consequences

- Good: product can roll out without deploys.
- Bad: another vendor in the critical path of every request; SDKs cache flags locally to limit the blast radius.
