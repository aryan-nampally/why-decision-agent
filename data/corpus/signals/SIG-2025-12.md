title: Peak season traffic report
date: 2025-12-02
source: "Capacity review"
author: Priya Raman
---

Cyber Monday freight surge: ledger writes peaked at 3,140 per second on December 1 between 14:00 and 15:00 UTC, about 5x our 2023 peak. The ledger primary (db.r6g.4xlarge) hit 88% CPU and p99 commit latency rose from 9 ms to 140 ms during the peak hour. FP&A's 2026 model projects 4,500 writes per second at next year's peak.
