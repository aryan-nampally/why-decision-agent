date: 2025-01-20

We are live in Europe. Twelve shippers in Germany, the Netherlands and Poland are onboarded, served by new frontends and tracking in eu-central-1 (Frankfurt). EU customers are billed and quoted in EUR. Payments, the ledger and all back-office systems remain in us-east-1 for now. Sales expects EU to reach 20% of quote volume by the end of 2025.
