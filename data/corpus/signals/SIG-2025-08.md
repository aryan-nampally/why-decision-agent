title: Finance reporting moved to Snowflake
date: 2025-08-14
source: "Weekly ops review"
author: Dmitri Volkov
---

Month-end close now runs entirely in Snowflake. Fivetran replicates ledger, invoice and account tables via change-data-capture with about 5 minutes of lag, and all 40 close reports have been rewritten in dbt. Per the DBA policy agreed with Payments, ad-hoc and reporting queries against the production ledger primary are now disabled; only the application service accounts may connect.
