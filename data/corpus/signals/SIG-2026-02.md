title: Enterprise deals now require SOC 2 Type II
date: 2026-02-17
source: "Sales & Security sync"
author: Aisha Siddiqui
---

Three enterprise prospects in the current pipeline, worth about $2.1M ARR combined, have made SOC 2 Type II attestation a contract requirement. Security is targeting an audit window in Q3 2026. The auditor has flagged identity and access management as an in-scope control area, including tamper-evident audit logs for administrative actions.
