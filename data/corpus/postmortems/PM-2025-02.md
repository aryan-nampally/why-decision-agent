# PM-2025-02: Wrong-currency and stale quotes for EU shippers after Frankfurt launch

## Summary

Three weeks after EU shippers were onboarded, a German shipper reported that the portal quoted a Hamburg–Rotterdam lane in USD and that the price changed at booking. Investigation found two problems: quotes for EU shippers were served from the us-east-1 cache with p95 latency of 110 ms, and the cache key had no currency component, so EUR requests could receive cached USD prices for the same lane and weight band.

## Timeline (UTC)

- 2025-01-20 EU shippers onboarded to the portal via eu-central-1 frontends.
- 2025-02-06 First customer report of a USD quote.
- 2025-02-07 Confirmed cache key collision between currencies.
- 2025-02-10 Hotfix adds currency to cache key; hit rate falls from 85% to 71%.

## Root cause

The quote cache was designed for customers who are all in North America and priced in USD. EU shippers broke both conditions: they are far from us-east-1, and they are priced in EUR.

## Impact

- 1,140 EU quotes shown in the wrong currency; 37 bookings re-priced at confirmation.
- p95 quote latency for EU shippers 110 ms, against a 40 ms target.

## Action items

1. Currency in cache key (done).
2. Evaluate a regional cache in eu-central-1.
