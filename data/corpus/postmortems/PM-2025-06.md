# PM-2025-06: Three-hour tracking delay from Kafka consumer lag

## Summary

Tracking updates in the shipper portal lagged by up to three hours on a Sunday. A partition reassignment after an MSK broker replacement left one consumer group rebalancing in a loop.

## Timeline (UTC)

- 03:10 MSK replaces broker 2 after a hardware fault.
- 03:25 `tracking-consumer` group enters repeated rebalances; lag climbs past 400k messages.
- 04:40 On-call (Tracking) escalates to Platform. Marcus Oyelaran is the only engineer with Kafka experience and is on leave; escalation takes 70 minutes.
- 05:50 Marcus joins, increases `max.poll.interval.ms`, pins partition assignment.
- 06:15 Lag drains.

```
2025-06-22T03:41:09Z WARN tracking-consumer rebalance in progress generation=184 members=6
2025-06-22T04:02:55Z WARN tracking-consumer lag=412933 partition=7
```

## Root cause

A long GC pause during reassignment caused the consumer to exceed `max.poll.interval.ms`. The runbook did not cover this failure mode, and the team's Kafka expertise now rests on a single person.

## Action items

1. Update runbook for rebalance loops.
2. Kafka training for two more platform engineers.
3. Consider vendor support for MSK.
