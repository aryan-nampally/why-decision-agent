# PM-2025-09: Terraform state lock left behind after CI runner crash

## Summary

A CI runner was terminated mid-apply, leaving the DynamoDB lock for the staging network state in place. Deploys to staging were blocked for 90 minutes until the lock was removed manually.

## Root cause

The CI job did not trap SIGTERM to release the lock. The lock and state design itself worked as intended.

## Action items

1. Trap termination signals in CI apply jobs.
2. Document safe force-unlock procedure.
